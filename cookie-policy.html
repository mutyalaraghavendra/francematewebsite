Cookie Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you browse websites. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the owners of the site.

2. What Cookies FranceMate Uses

At our current launch phase, FranceMate primarily uses essential cookies. These are strictly necessary to provide you with services available through our website and to use some of its features.

If you give us your consent via the cookie banner, we may also use analytics cookies (such as Google Analytics) to help us understand how visitors interact with our website, so we can improve the user experience.

3. How to Manage Cookies

You can set your browser to refuse all or some browser cookies, or to alert you when websites set or access cookies. If you disable or refuse cookies, please note that some parts of this website may become inaccessible or not function properly.

4. Your Consent Choices

When you first visit our website, you will see a cookie banner offering you the choice to accept or decline non-essential cookies. You can change your preferences at any time by clearing your browser cookies and refreshing the page.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. The updated version will be indicated by the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about our use of cookies, please contact us at contact@francemate.com.
