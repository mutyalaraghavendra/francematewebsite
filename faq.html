Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about FranceMate and how we help students.

What is FranceMate? ?

FranceMate is a student assistance service founded by 4 EPITA students to help international students settle in France.

Who can use FranceMate? ?

International students arriving in France, particularly Paris.

Do you help international students? ?

Yes, that is our entire focus.

Do you provide airport pickup? ?

Yes, we provide airport arrival coordination from CDG and Orly.

Which airports do you cover? ?

Paris CDG and Paris Orly.

Do you cover CDG? ?

Yes, Paris Charles de Gaulle Airport.

Do you cover Orly? ?

Yes, Paris Orly Airport.

Do you cover Beauvais? ?

No, we currently serve CDG and Orly only.

What does the 35 package include? ?

It includes all 12 items listed in the pricing section, serving as an administrative and settlement guide. Note that any third-party costs for actual services (like rent, transport tickets, SIM card costs) are not included.

Can FranceMate help with housing? ?

We provide guidance only to help you navigate your options. We don't act as a landlord.

Can FranceMate help with CAF? ?

We provide guidance only. Final decisions and disbursements are made by CAF.

Can FranceMate help with Social Security? ?

General guidance is provided; all official decisions are by CPAM.

Can FranceMate help me get a SIM card? ?

Yes, we offer guidance on selecting and setting up SIM card options.

Can FranceMate help me open a bank account? ?

We provide guidance on options, not financial advice, and there is no guaranteed approval.

Can FranceMate help me find a part-time job? ?

Yes, we can provide tips on where and how to look, but no employment is guaranteed.

Do you guarantee jobs? ?

No.

Do you provide immigration/legal advice? ?

No, we are not qualified legal advisors.

Are you affiliated with the French government? ?

No, we are an independent student assistance service.

Coming to France?

You don't have to figure everything out alone.

FranceMate is here to help international students settle into France with confidence.
