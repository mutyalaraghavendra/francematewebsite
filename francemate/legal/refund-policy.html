Refund Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

This Refund Policy outlines the terms under which refunds may be issued for the services provided by FranceMate. We aim to be fair and transparent in all our dealings with students.

2. What the 35 Fee Covers

The 35 fee covers our time, effort, and personalized guidance provided as part of our core assistance package. This includes helping you navigate processes like opening a bank account, setting up a SIM card, applying for CAF, and general settlement advice.

3. Cancellation Before Service Delivery

If you cancel your request for our services before we have commenced providing any assistance or guidance, you are eligible for a full refund of the 35 fee. Please notify us as soon as possible if you decide not to proceed.

4. Cancellation After Service Has Commenced

Once we have begun providing our services (e.g., we have started answering your specific queries, provided tailored resources, or begun assisting with administrative tasks), the 35 fee becomes non-refundable. The fee compensates us for the time and effort already expended.

5. Non-Refundable Third-Party Costs

Please note that any third-party costs you incur are strictly non-refundable by FranceMate. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Transport costs (trains, buses, Navigo passes).
  • SIM card purchases or mobile plan fees.
  • Housing deposits, rent, or agency fees.
  • Government processing fees or insurance premiums.

6. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund (if eligible under Section 3), please send an email to contact@francemate.com with the subject line "Refund Request". Include your full name, the date you paid, and the reason for your request.

7. Processing Time

Approved refunds will be processed within 7-10 business days and will be returned via the original method of payment.

8. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our Refund Policy, please contact us at contact@francemate.com.
