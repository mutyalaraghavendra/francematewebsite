All Student Services in France

From landing at the airport to getting your first part-time job, we've got you covered.

Our Comprehensive Services

We provide a full range of services to help you navigate French bureaucracy and settle in seamlessly.

Airport Pickup

Don't get lost on your first day. Our drivers wait for you at CDG or Orly and take you directly to your accommodation safely.

Student Housing

Finding a place in France is hard. We assist you with guarantors, lease agreements, and finding reliable landlords.

SIM Card setup

Get connected on day one. We guide you to the best student mobile plans with plenty of data for cheap.

Bank Account Setup

You need a French RIB for everything. We help you open a student bank account quickly with zero stress.

Home & Civil Insurance

Mandatory for renting in France. We'll set up your comprehensive housing and civil liability insurance.

Social Security

Navigate the CPAM registration to get your healthcare costs reimbursed and receive your Carte Vitale.

CAF Application

Apply for housing financial assistance. We guide you through the French forms to ensure maximum chances of approval.

Transport Setup

We'll help you get your Navigo Imagine R pass for unlimited travel around the le-de-France region.

Student Jobs

Need extra cash? We'll review your CV, practice interview skills in French, and point you to the best job boards.
