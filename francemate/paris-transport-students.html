Paris Transport Guide for International Students

Mastering the Metro, RER, and Navigo card.

Overview of Paris Public Transport

The transport network in Paris and the le-de-France region is extensive and efficient, operated primarily by RATP and SNCF under the umbrella of le-de-France Mobilits. The city is divided into 5 zones (Zones 1-2 cover central Paris, Zones 3-5 cover the suburbs and airports).

The Metro & RER

The Metro consists of 16 lines running predominantly within central Paris (Zones 1-2). It's the most common way to get around quickly.

The RER (Rseau Express Rgional) has 5 lines (A, B, C, D, E) and functions as an express train connecting central Paris to the broader suburbs. Key lines for students include RER B (connects to CDG Airport and southern suburbs) and RER A (connects major business and university hubs).

Navigo Pass & Student Discounts (Imagine R)

For regular travel, you need a Navigo card. You can opt for weekly or monthly passes. However, if you are a student under 26, the best option is the Navigo Imagine R student pass. It offers unlimited travel across all 5 zones for a heavily discounted yearly fee (around 350/year).

Ticket Types

  • Single Tickets (t+): Good for occasional travel.
  • Navigo Libert+: Pay as you go, deducting from your bank account monthly.
  • Navigo Monthly/Weekly: Unlimited travel for a set duration.

Getting from the Airport

From CDG: The fastest way is the RER B train to central Paris. Taxis offer fixed rates to the Right or Left Bank. We also offer private airport pickup for peace of mind!

From Orly: You can take the Orlyval to RER B, the Tram T7, or specific shuttle buses like OrlyBus.

Other Transport Options & Tips

Paris is very bike-friendly. You can use Vlib' (the city's bike-sharing program) which has cheap student subscriptions. Also, make sure to download navigation apps like Citymapper or Google Maps, and SNCF Connect to manage your digital tickets.

Need help settling in?

We can help you order your Imagine R card, find your way from the airport, and get your SIM set up.
