❓ Any Questions?

Have a Doubt? We've Got You.

Common questions from students planning to arrive in France.

How do I book a service with FranceMate? ▼
Simply fill in our contact form, tell us which services you need and your arrival date. We'll confirm your booking within 24 hours via email or WhatsApp.
Does the €50 cover everything I need? ▼
The €50 is our service fee for guidance and coordination across all areas. It does not include third-party costs like transportation fares, SIM card charges, bank fees or insurance premiums — those are communicated separately before any purchase.
I'm arriving at CDG — can FranceMate help? ▼
Yes! We provide airport arrival coordination for both Paris Charles de Gaulle (CDG) and Paris Orly (ORY). Book before your flight and we'll send you pickup instructions.
I don't speak French — is that okay? ▼
Absolutely. FranceMate was built for international students. We communicate in English and help you navigate French administrative procedures step by step.
How quickly will I get a response? ▼
We aim to respond within 24 hours via WhatsApp or email. For urgent queries before your arrival, WhatsApp is the fastest way to reach us.
Can I book just one service, or do I need the full package? ▼
You can book any individual service — airport pickup, housing guidance, SIM help, or anything else. The €50 starter package covers all services together for the best value.
