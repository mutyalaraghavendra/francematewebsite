CAF & Housing Assistance for Students in France

Learn how to get financial help with your student rent in France.

What is CAF?

The CAF (Caisse d'Allocations Familiales) is a French government organization that provides various financial aids, including housing assistance, to residents in Franceincluding international students!

Types of Housing Assistance

Depending on your accommodation, you may be eligible for one of these subsidies:

  • APL (Aide Personnalise au Logement): Usually for students living in conventioned student residences (like CROUS).
  • ALS (Allocation de Logement Social): Commonly given to students renting private apartments or shared flats.
  • ALF (Allocation de Logement Familial): Less common for single students, generally for those with dependents.

Eligibility Considerations

Not everyone automatically receives CAF. Eligibility varies based on several factors:

  • Immigration Status: You must have a valid residence permit or validated long-stay visa (VLS-TS).
  • Accommodation Type: Your name must be on the lease (bail) and the housing must meet certain decent living standards.
  • Income: It is means-tested, meaning the amount you receive depends on your income (student incomes usually qualify for maximum allowed aid).

Documents Students May Need

When applying, you'll generally be asked to provide:

  • A signed rental lease (Bail) in your name
  • An "Attestation de Loyer" completed by your landlord
  • French Bank Details (RIB)
  • Passport and Birth Certificate
  • Validated Student Visa / Titre de Sjour
  • Proof of Student Status (Certificate of enrollment)

General Application Process & Common Delays

You must apply online on the official CAF website after you have moved into your apartment. A common mistake is waiting too longbenefits only start the month after you move in and apply, and they are not retroactive.

Expect delays; it can take several months for your file to be processed, but you will receive back pay for eligible months once approved.

Important Disclaimer: FranceMate provides guidance on the application process, but we cannot guarantee that you will receive CAF. Eligibility and benefit decisions are made entirely by the CAF authorities.

CAF Application Help

The CAF portal is entirely in French and can be confusing. We help you prepare your documents and fill out the forms correctly.
