Airport Pickup

Paris Airport Pickup for International Students

Landing in Paris for the first time? FranceMate helps international students coordinate their arrival from CDG or Orly to their accommodation.

Supported Airports

Paris Charles de Gaulle CDG

We provide coordination from Charles de Gaulle (CDG) airport, one of the main international gateways to Paris. CDG is large and complex, with three main terminals (T1, T2, T3) and multiple sub-terminals in T2. Our service helps you navigate the typical arrival process, find your way out, and meet your coordinated transport.

Paris Orly ORY

Paris Orly (ORY) is the second largest airport serving Paris, often used for European or North African flights. It has four terminals (Orly 1, 2, 3, 4). We coordinate pickups from all Orly terminals directly to your student housing, ensuring a smooth transition into Paris.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which airports do you cover? ?
We cover the major Paris airports: Charles de Gaulle (CDG) and Orly (ORY).
Do you cover CDG? ?
Yes, we provide Charles de Gaulle student transfer services from all CDG terminals.
Do you cover Orly? ?
Yes, we cover all terminals at Paris Orly (ORY) for Orly to Paris student transfers.
How do I book? ?
You can start by contacting us through our website. We'll guide you through the booking process.
What information do I need to provide? ?
We will need your flight number, arrival date and time, airline, and the full address of your accommodation in Paris.
Is pickup available 24/7? ?
Pickup availability depends on our transport partners. Please contact us with your specific flight details to confirm.

Disclaimer: The 35 FranceMate service fee covers our assistance and coordination services. It does not include the cost of the actual third-party transport (e.g., taxi, VTC, shuttle) from the airport to your accommodation. You will need to pay for the transport separately.
