Our Story

FranceMate was created by four students who understand what it feels like to arrive in a new country and figure everything out from scratch.

We are Raghavendra, Sanjeev, Karthik and Arun four students studying at EPITA in France.

We realized that international students often spend countless hours trying to understand housing, SIM cards, banking, insurance, Social Security, transportation, administration and part-time jobs.

We created FranceMate to make that journey simpler. Our mission is to become the first trusted friend every international student has when they arrive in France.
